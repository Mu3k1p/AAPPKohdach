Our story

Born between the river and the leaves.

Koh Dach is only a short ride from Phnom Penh, but it runs on a different clock. Weavers work silk by hand, bikes rattle down dirt lanes and the Mekong keeps moving at its own pace.

Amra was made for that pace. A place to sit under plants, order something good and let an hour disappear without feeling bad about it.

Getting here

Amra is on Koh Dach, also called Silk Island, in Kandal Province. Drive or ride out of Phnom Penh, then cross to the island by ferry. Allow a relaxed hour, more on weekends.
